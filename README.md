# Data Analysis Project Summary:

The "Sales Dashboard South America 2022" is an Excel-based analytical project designed to visualize and interpret McDonald's sales performance across South America for the year 2022. The dashboard aggregates key business metrics, including sales, profits, the number of customers, and satisfaction indicators, into a concise, interactive format.

# Key Sections and Findings:

1. Overview Metrics: The top section displays key highlights for the year: Sales at 85%, Profit at 89%, and Number of Customers at 87%, presented via scorecard-style donut charts, indicating target achievement relative to set goals.

2. Sales Trend Analysis: A line chart shows monthly sales data from 2021 versus 2022. The 2022 trend (red) generally outperforms 2021 (yellow), especially in Q4 with sales peaking in December, denoting seasonal growth and successful strategic initiatives.

3. Geographical Insights: A shaded map of South America breaks down 2022 sales by country. Darker shades represent higher sales, with standout performance in select nations, helping to pinpoint geographic strongholds and market opportunities.

4. Customer Satisfaction: A radar chart evaluates customer perceptions across five criteria: Speed (54%), Service (53%), Availability (95%), Quality (86%), and Hygiene (93%). High scores in Availability, Hygiene, and Quality highlight operational strengths, while lower scores in Speed and Service suggest areas for operational improvement.

5. Dashboard Usability: The structured layout makes complex data easy to interpret for stakeholders, supporting executive decisions on where to invest and where to improve. The left navigation panel hints at further interactive capabilities typical of an Excel dashboard.

# Project Value:
This project demonstrates the power of Excel in compiling, visualizing, and analyzing multidimensional sales data—enabling performance tracking, strategic planning, and focused customer experience improvements for McDonald's in South America. The clear graphical presentation and breakdown of KPIs, trends, and satisfaction metrics make it suitable for both quick executive overviews and deeper analytical exploration.
